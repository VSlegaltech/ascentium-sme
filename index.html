FAQ

Questions founders ask us

Who is my contract with?

Your engagement is with Ascentium in your jurisdiction. Ascentium is responsible for the service and for any statutory roles such as company secretary. The platform is powered by Zolvit.

Does AI make decisions about my filings?

No. AI reads documents, matches transactions, tracks deadlines and answers questions. A qualified professional reviews and signs off every filing before it is submitted.

Who is Ascentia?

Ascentia is our AI voice assistant. Call or tap to talk any time to check your status, ask about a deadline or request a change. When something needs a professional’s judgement, she brings in your named expert with the full context.

Can I switch from Sleek or another provider?

Yes. We handle the handover of registers and records, import your accounting history from Xero, QuickBooks or spreadsheets, and file the change of secretary or address for you.

Which countries do you cover?

The SME platform runs in 21 markets across Asia Pacific, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas. Larger groups can use all 27 Ascentium markets through Ascentium for Enterprise.

Who qualifies for SME fares?

Companies that meet the official SME definition where they are registered, assessed together with any group companies. Use the eligibility checker to see your result in seconds.

Can I incorporate without visiting Singapore or Hong Kong?

Yes. Identity checks and signatures are done online. In Singapore, non-resident founders will need a local resident director, which we can provide as a nominee.

How long does incorporation take?

Once documents and KYC are complete, a Singapore company is typically registered within one to three business days and a Hong Kong company within about a week, subject to registry processing.
